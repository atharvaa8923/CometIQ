BUAN 6356 — Business Analytics with R
Naveen Jindal School of Management · Spring 2026 · 3 credit hours
Instructor: Dr. Morgan Lee · Office hours: Tue 2–4 pm (JSOM 2.101)

COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course introduces the use of R for business analytics. Students learn to import, clean and transform business data, explore it with descriptive statistics and data visualization, and build regression and classification models to support managerial decisions. Emphasis is on reproducible analysis in RStudio with the tidyverse and on communicating results to business stakeholders.

PREREQUISITES
OPRE 6301 Quantitative Introduction to Risk and Uncertainty in Business (statistics).

LEARNING OBJECTIVES
By the end of the course students will be able to:
1. Use R programming and RStudio to import and manipulate data (data wrangling with dplyr and tidyr).
2. Apply data cleaning techniques to handle missing values, outliers and inconsistent records.
3. Summarize data using descriptive statistics and probability concepts; conduct hypothesis testing.
4. Build and interpret linear regression and logistic regression models.
5. Create effective data visualization with ggplot2 and communicate insights in written reports and presentations.
6. Apply classification trees and clustering (data mining) to segment customers.
